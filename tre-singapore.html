Logo

A blue globe with bronze continents, an eagle in flight, the italic "TRE" with its tagline, a navy rule and "Singapore" underneath. Built as an SVG, so it scales cleanly.

Primary · SVG, any sizeSVG
Raster · PNG for docs and CanvaPNG
On navy · always on a white panelSVG
Do
  • Prefer the SVG; it holds every detail at any size.
  • Keep "Singapore" and the rule attached: it is the local identity.
  • Minimum width 140 px so the tagline stays readable.
Don't
  • Recolour the globe or the bronze eagle.
  • Crop the tagline off to make it fit.
  • Put the full-colour logo straight on navy or a photo.

Open item: the logo artwork shows the official TRE® mark, while all text across the three sites uses TRE™ (client rule, Aug 2026). Leave the logo as is and confirm with Isabelle if a ™ version is wanted.

Voice & copy

Plain-language and welcoming, for first-timers and professionals alike. Make TRE™ easy to find, safe to learn and rewarding to teach.

Release stress. Strengthen resilience. Improve wellbeing.Brand line, tre-in-singapore.com

Write it like this

  • Explain the name once: Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises
  • Speak to four audiences: providers, trainees, allied professionals, first-timers
  • "Certified provider", "Global TRE™ Provider Certification"

Avoid

  • Promising clinical outcomes
  • TRE® or a bare "TRE" in text
  • Publishing 2027 certification dates or prices before the client signs off

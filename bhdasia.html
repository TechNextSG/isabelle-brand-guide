01

Logo

The mark is a sun-yellow sail over a marigold B and D, split by thin navy and red bars. The wordmark line "Business and Human Development" sits underneath.

Do
  • Keep clear space of at least the height of the "B" bowl on every side.
  • Minimum size: 64 px wide on screen, 18 mm in print.
  • On dark or busy photos, place the mark on a white rounded tile.
  • In co-branded pieces BHD Asia sits top left and leads.
Don't
  • Recolour, outline or add shadows to the bars.
  • Set the full-colour mark straight on navy or a photo.
  • Stretch it: the file is square (1:1).
  • Re-typeset the wordmark line in another font.

Asset gap: the only logo file is a 500×500 raster. Ask Isabelle for the SVG or a large original before any print job.

02

Colour

Taken straight from the logo. Warm light surfaces carry most of the page; marigold is the action colour. Red and navy appear only as the thin two-bar accent, at most once per layout. Click a swatch to copy it.

Rough proportion on a typical page.

Contrast: marigold fails as text on white. Use ink text on marigold buttons, and amber-ink #8F5A00 when you need orange text on a light background.

04

Photography

Real people, real rooms. Isabelle's professional portraits lead; workshop photos show the work happening. Avoid generic stock boardrooms.

05

Voice & copy

Confident, warm and specific. Talk about people and organisations, not products.

Build stronger leaders. Develop resilient organisations.Homepage headline, bhdasia.com

1 · Organisation Development

Leadership programme design, culture change, team coaching, talent management, effectiveness diagnostics.

2 · Resilience Building

TRE™, stress management, burnout prevention, conflict resolution, psychological safety.

3 · Individual Development

Executive and leadership coaching for individuals.

Write it like this

  • "Co-designed with your people"
  • Short sentences, sentence case
  • TRE™ with ™, every time it appears
  • Team named as "BHD Asia team members"

Avoid

  • TRE® or a bare "TRE" in text
  • Hype words: "revolutionary", "game-changing"
  • Prices in event hero stats
  • The private phone number on legal pages

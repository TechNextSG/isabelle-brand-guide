Do
  • Use the horizontal lock-up in headers and next to partner logos; the stacked one for squares and avatars.
  • Use the dark-background file on navy, the light one everywhere else.
  • Keep clear space of at least one wing-tip width around it.
Don't
  • Two-tone the wordmark on thumbnails: "HummingBeing" stays all gold.
  • Separate the bird from the wordmark in co-branded pieces.
  • Place it on busy photos without a navy or cream panel behind it.

Write it like this

  • "Trauma-informed", "body-led", "science-backed"

  • TRE™ = Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises, by Dr David Berceli

Avoid

  • Medical claims ("heals trauma", "cures anxiety")
  • TRE® or a bare "TRE" in text
  • Emoji in testimonials and headings
